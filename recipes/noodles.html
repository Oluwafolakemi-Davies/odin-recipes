Noodles

Description

Noodles are the ultimate comfort food, providing a quick yet incredibly satisfying meal that can be customized in a hundred different ways. This recipe focuses on a "stir-fry" style, where the noodles are tossed with fresh vegetables and a punchy spice blend.

Whether you're a student in a hurry or looking for a late-night snack, these noodles hit the spot by balancing savory and spicy notes. Adding a fried egg or shredded cabbage transforms these simple grains into a complete, balanced feast.

Ingredients

  • 2 packs of instant noodles (any flavor)
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 carrot and a handful of green beans (chopped)
  • 2 fresh chili peppers (minced)
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large egg
  • Noodle seasoning packs

Steps

  1. Parboil: Boil the noodles in water for 2 to 3 minutes until they are nearly soft, then drain the water and set aside.
  2. Sauté: Heat oil in a pan and sauté the onions, peppers, and vegetables until they are slightly tender.
  3. Scramble: Crack the egg into the pan and scramble it gently with the vegetables.
  4. Combine: Add the cooked noodles and the seasoning packs to the pan.
  5. Toss: Stir everything together over high heat for 1 minute to ensure the spices are evenly distributed.
